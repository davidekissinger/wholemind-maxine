# Archival supplement — Maxine and Robert Earl Boggs

Added: October 28, 2026. Status: review site source record.
Scope: The Maxine Project only. No change to WholeMind Living's content.

## Original family-held materials

1. Doris M. Boggs memorial obituary: founder-supplied photograph of a printed tribute stated to have appeared in *The Elkhart Truth*, Elkhart, Indiana, May 24, 2000. The print identifies Doris Maxine Boggs (April 5, 1922 – May 22, 2000), born in Brush, Colorado, daughter of Charles and Henrietta (Johnson) Ratliff. It records her marriage to John R. Boggs Jr. on July 10, 1943; her involvement in ministry, Child Evangelism Clubs, and United Methodist Women; and family survivors including daughters Carol Van Pelt and Barbara McMunn, six grandchildren, and four great-grandchildren. It is a memorial obituary, **not clinical evidence** of an Alzheimer's diagnosis.
2. Robert Earl Boggs newspaper obituary: founder-supplied photograph of an undated clipping headed "Robert Earl Boggs." It records Robert's age (19), death on Sunday, July 5 in a collision on Indiana State Road 9 near Marion, his Parker High School salutatorian distinction in 1963, US Navy enlistment in June 1963 and training at Great Lakes, assignment in Philadelphia, intention to enter the ministry after his Navy service, sisters Betty, Carol and Barbara, and announced burial at Lindenwood Cemetery. The clipping's year and publication are not visible. Sunday July 5 was in **1964**, a plausible year given the other facts, but this is **inference**, not a printed date.
3. Studio portrait: founder-supplied photo of a print of Maxine in glasses with a pink corsage, photographer and date unknown. A cropped, compressed derivative is published only on the Maxine brand's memorial page, with attribution and alt text. The original photo remains in the conversation archive, not the repository.

## Source integrity

- The May 24, 2000 *Elkhart Truth* memorial uses the birth surname **Ratliff**. The previously cited Asbury alumni obituary uses **Ratcliff**. The spelling is **not reconciled**; the approved memorial heading is retained pending verification.
- The earlier short *Star Press* "Mother dies" death notice did **not** print the name Barbara; the newly supplied longer memorial **does** identify Barbara McMunn. The two different reports are labeled separately.
- The obituary photographs are **not published or fully transcribed**. Only factual summaries with explicit provenance appear in HTML. Possessing a newspaper clipping does not necessarily convey its copyright.
- The family portrait is shared at the founder's request; photographer attribution and underlying copyright are not independently established.
- Dates, burial announcements, and personal histories in obituaries are attributed to those publications, not presented as verified civil, military, burial, or medical records.
