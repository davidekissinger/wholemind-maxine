## Editorial purpose

This is a second narrative pillar after \`Meet Maxine\`, dedicated to the
founder's grandfather **Rev. John Robert Boggs Jr.**, who wrote under
**J. Robert Boggs**. It expands the existing shortened family-account
section at \`story.html#family-story\`, which remains live and linked.

The layout reuses the established The Maxine Project typographic,
plum-accent, light/dark design language. The book panel is typographic
only and is clearly **not** original cover artwork.

## Source map and levels of confidence

**Family testimony supplied by founder David E. Kissinger:**
- Maxine is his grandmother, Rev. John Robert Boggs Jr. his grandfather.
- The founder reports his grandfather cared for Maxine for **more than two decades**.
- The founder identifies his grandfather's book, \`I'll Move Over\`, as
  an account of the family's experience with Alzheimer's disease.
- The meaning assigned to their story for The Maxine Project is editorial
  interpretation of the founder's perspective, not a quotation from the book.

These are family statements; no independent caregiving-duration record
has been established. See \`docs/family-story-sources.md\`.

**Institutional biographical account:** Asbury Theological Seminary,
"Alumni Obituaries — August 2013," July 25, 2013:
https://alumni.asburyseminary.edu/elink/alumni-obituaries-august-2013/
Supports the marriage to Maxine Ratcliff on July 10, 1943, Maxine's
death in May 2000, Rev. Boggs's service as a United Methodist minister
beginning in 1944, his retirement in 1992, ministry in Ohio, North
Dakota, and Indiana, and a soup kitchen established in Elkhart.
This memorial does not independently document his caregiving duration
or the contents of his book.

**Credential verification (October 25, 2026):**
- Asbury Theological Seminary's 2013 alumni memorial specifically
  identifies Rev. John Robert Boggs Jr. as **"(1949, BD)"**:
  https://alumni.asburyseminary.edu/elink/alumni-obituaries-august-2013/
  Present his actual award as **Bachelor of Divinity (BD), 1949**,
  never as an MDiv earned in 1949.

## Claims intentionally omitted or qualified

- **No claim to have read the dedication or full book.** The supplied
  draft's reference to the recorded dedication was omitted until the
  text can be examined.
- **No characterization of specific chapters, quoted passages, or
  illustrations.** An author's experiences are discussed using
  founder-reported family history, not fabricated citations.
- **No assertion that his role was exclusively "primary caregiver"**
  when the independently available biographical records do not specify it.
- **No reproduction of book cover, book content, family photographs,
  or newspaper clipping.** Permissions and provenance need review
  before these materials could be included.
- **No claims of nonprofit incorporation/exemption, an official
  research collaboration, treatment efficacy, paid book sales, or
  publishing rights.**
