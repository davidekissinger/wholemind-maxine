## Scope of the historical document

- **Page 1:** A handwritten poem/tribute to the pastor signed Vicki Bridenstine, dated **June 1984**. This is part of the scrapbook, not proof the whole retirement occurred in 1984.
- **Page 2:** A ministerial photograph, Grace United Methodist Church photo, church address, and a printed statement signed "Brother Bob Boggs, Pastor." Source image is not reproduced in this edition.
- **Pages 3–4:** Retirement greeting card.
- **Pages 5–9:** Illustration of student and full-time appointments, spanning 1944 through a Grace United Methodist Church appointment labeled **1981–1985**.
- **Pages 10–14:** Additional cards, Grace church-events chronology and photographs, including June 7, 1981 first Sunday (p. 11); July 10, 1983 fortieth wedding anniversary (p. 12); and May–June 1985 farewell events (p. 13).
- **Pages 15–17:** Affectionate humorous multi-page verse, "Hats Off to Boggs", and a pasted item from **The Elkhart Truth, May 31, 1985**, titled "Dinner Given in Honor of Rev. and Mrs. J. Robert Boggs." Do not treat jokes or anecdotes in the verse as independently corroborated.
- **Page 18:** An uncaptained photograph of a couple, plausibly Bob and Maxine in this family context. The scan itself does not label the subjects or date. Confirm identity and rights before providing a caption or reusing it.

## Details used on the website

The family-held scrapbook documents these milestones:

1. The July 10, 1943 marriage is also independently given in Asbury Theological Seminary's alumni memorial; the 1983 scrapbook entry observes their fortieth wedding anniversary.
2. The illustrated appointment history places his student ministry in Ohio and full-time service in North Dakota and Indiana. Spellings and assignments have not been reconciled with denominational records. Page 5 seems to print "Bowerville" rather than Bowersville.
3. The Grace church-event chronology labels June 7, 1981 as his first Sunday and records ongoing pastoral and congregational activities through spring 1985.
4. The newspaper clipping **reports** May 24, 1985 as the retirement dinner at Eby Pines Restaurant, Bristol, Indiana, and explicitly honors Rev. **and Mrs.** J. Robert Boggs. The clipping identifies a name quilt, a United Methodist Women's history book, and a scrapbook as gifts.
5. The document records a June farewell reception. The scrapbook's dated events list says June 5, 1985; the May 31 newspaper notice describes an upcoming **Sunday** reception, which would be June 2. Do not choose a definitive day without further source work.
6. The three-page poem presents Bob's hospitality, practical help, hobbies, and humor from the viewpoint of an affectionate congregation. Attribution should always be to that tribute.

No original text beyond short identifying titles is reproduced. Paraphrases of the article's factual content are attributable to the article; interpretation of the significance for Maxine belongs to website editorial writing.

## Conflict: 1985 vs. 1992

**Do not overwrite one record with the other.** The scrapbook documents farewell/retirement events related to Grace UMC in **1985**. The Asbury Theological Seminary alumni obituary for Rev. John Robert Boggs Jr. (1949, BD) states that his ministry began in 1944 and he **retired in 1992**.

Institutional source:
https://alumni.asburyseminary.edu/elink/alumni-obituaries-august-2013/

The precise distinction between the 1985 and 1992 dates is unresolved. In public copy say "1985 farewell from Grace" rather than definitively stating that formal ministry ended in 1985. The website's existing broader biography retains the explicitly attributed 1992 date.
