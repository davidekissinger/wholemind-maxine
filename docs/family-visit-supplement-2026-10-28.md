# Family-visit supplement — editorial and privacy record

Date: October 28, 2026.
Scope: The Maxine Project only. Proposed for the existing review/noindex site.

## Origin of these amendments

The founder revisited family photographs during an October 28, 2026 visit and supplied a private recording, transcript, and informal summary for review. The transcript is a recollection source, not a contemporaneous medical record, and automatic speaker identification is imperfect.

- **Meet Maxine:** Clarifies that the age of about 57 at the beginning of noticed changes comes from a family memory, not a documented onset date. Adds a brief first-person account of remembering the Lake James years, without assigning an exact date or offering clinical conclusions.
- **The Family Account:** Adds the founder's memories of rooms in the Elkhart home, sermon preparation, and two doves his grandfather used in children's magic shows. These details are labeled as memories and are not represented as quotations from *I'll Move Over*.
- **Free resources:** Adds *Preserving Their Story*, a browser-printable guide for keeping photograph identifications and family recollections alongside their provenance. It has no downloadable PDF in this release; the other six PDFs remain unchanged.

## Verification and limitations

The May 24, 2000 memorial obituary and the existing Asbury source remain the cited historical sources for Maxine's life dates and her husband's service. The family conversation does not independently prove a diagnosis date, symptom-onset year, property chronology, or medical cause.

No other relative is identified or quoted from the recording. The private audio and transcript are **not** copied into this public repository. No additional photographs, scans of newspaper clippings, or book content are added. The existing family-supplied portrait remains the only published photo of Maxine.

The photograph guide asks for permission before copying, recording, or publishing material. Borrowing a family album does not, by itself, confer publication rights.

## Excluded material

Contemporary personal or medical matters involving living relatives, the unrelated Delta property, and personal family career histories remain outside this public website update. They may be evaluated separately in a private family-history archive, with permission where appropriate.

## Release boundaries

Preserve review/noindex, the existing site disclaimers and privacy statements, and separation between the two brands. This update does not change the Research & Innovation pages, imply a medical study, collect visitor data, change nonprofit status, or add donations.
